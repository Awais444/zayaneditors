## Rules for every new article

- Title ≤ 60 chars, description ≤ 155, unique (checked by `npm run seo:check`).
- Answer the question in the first 100 words.
- Question-style H2s where natural; one comparison table where it helps.
- Link to its service page, `/pricing`, `/portfolio` and `/contact`.
- Author must be a real, verified team member before launch (see `seo-todo.md`).
